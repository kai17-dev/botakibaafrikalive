Artist Spotlight

Zanele Mahlangu

Zanele blends mbaqanga rhythm sections with contemporary production, writing lyrics in a mix of isiZulu and English. She's been recording with Botaki Ba Afrika's home-studio setup since 2023 and leads the collective's monthly live sessions.

I want people to hear their grandmother's kitchen and their phone's playlist in the same four bars.

Are you a musician?

We're looking for singers, instrumentalists, and producers to record, perform, and release with the collective.

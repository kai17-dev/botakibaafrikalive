Artist Spotlight

Naledi Khumalo

Naledi paints domestic interiors — kitchens, yards, half-lit hallways — treating the ordinary rooms of Black South African life as subjects worthy of a gallery wall. She has shown work at three Botaki Ba Afrika group exhibitions since joining the collective in 2024.

I paint the rooms nobody photographs. That's where the real story sits.

Are you a visual artist?

Botaki Ba Afrika is always looking for painters, illustrators, and mixed-media artists to join the collective's exhibitions and studio sessions.
